I'm Heba, School of Information Technology and Computer Science at Nile University. This blog collects notes from hands-on security and systems labs — environment setup, isolated vulnerability research, and the hardening steps that follow.

All testing documented here is performed in isolated, self-controlled environments (local VMs or private cloud instances bound to localhost) and is intended for learning and defensive hardening, not for use against systems you don't own or have explicit authorization to test.
